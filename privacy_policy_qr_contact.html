Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Chrome extension QR Contact Generator handles information when you use the extension.

1. What the extension does

The extension is designed to extract contact-related information from the webpage currently opened by the user, generate a vCard file, and create a QR code for easy contact import.

2. Information processed

When the user explicitly activates the extension, the extension may process content from the currently active webpage, including contact-related details such as name, organization, title, email address, phone number, postal address, website URLs, and similar business contact data available on that page.

3. How information is used

The processed webpage content is used solely to provide the requested functionality of the extension, namely:

  • extracting contact details from the active webpage,
  • generating a vCard contact file, and
  • generating a QR code representing the contact data.

4. Transmission to server

To generate the vCard and QR code, relevant webpage content may be transmitted securely to a remote server after explicit user action. This transmission is performed only to provide the requested functionality.

5. Data storage

The extension is not intended to permanently store personal data collected from webpages. Data sent to the server is processed only for generating the requested result and is not used for unrelated purposes.

6. Data sharing and sale

The processed data is not sold to third parties, is not used for advertising purposes, and is not used for cross-site tracking.

7. User control

The extension accesses webpage content only after explicit user interaction, such as clicking the extension button. The extension does not perform background monitoring of browsing activity.

8. Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect transmitted data during processing. However, no method of transmission or electronic processing is completely secure.

9. Third-party websites

The extension may process content from third-party websites selected by the user. This Privacy Policy applies only to the extension and not to the privacy practices of those websites.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
